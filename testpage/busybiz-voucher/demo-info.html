How it works

The customer plays a 30-second game on their phone, from a QR code on the table, a receipt or a social post.
At the end they enter name + email and tick a consent box.
The system checks the email hasn't claimed before, gives the next running number (BB-0001, BB-0002 …) and emails the voucher from the restaurant's Gmail.
At the counter, staff type the code into the redemption page. It shows who it belongs to and marks it used, so a voucher can't be used twice.
Every voucher is a row in your Google Sheet: who, when, which game, sent, redeemed.

Rules built in

✅ One voucher per email. Gmail tricks like dots and “+tags” count as the same address. A repeat player gets their original voucher re-sent instead.
✅ Running number on every voucher, never reused, even if rows are deleted.
✅ Expiry 30 days after issue, printed in the email and enforced at redemption.
✅ Single use. Redeeming marks the row, and a second attempt is refused.
✅ Optional cap on the total number of vouchers for a campaign.
